Read this before the numbers

The corpus is 1,389 English tokens in 50 paired passages. Published literary-corpus statistics usually run to hundreds of thousands of words; these are three orders of magnitude smaller, so treat every number as descriptive of this repository's text rather than of its authors.
The passages are hand-authored adaptations, not the complete works. Nothing here should be cited as a property of Dostoevsky or Proust.
Lexical overlap between the English and Italian versions is measured on token forms only, with no translation model. What it finds is proper nouns, cognates and loanwords - it is not semantic similarity.
Type/token ratio falls with length by arithmetic, so the single TTR figure is reported next to a TTR-against-length curve instead of on its own.
