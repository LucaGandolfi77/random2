Findings

A competent policy wins 100% of runsThe greedy policy is a rough stand-in for a player who picks well. If it wins nearly every level, the paxDay thresholds are not doing any work: the goal is a formality rather than a target. Compare with the actionsToWin column - if the goal falls in a handful of decisions, the real difficulty in the game is player attention, not the economy.
Day budgets are far larger than the time a goal needsMedian day at victory vs the level's day limit - fiume in 3 of 14 days; libera in 3 of 14 days; porto in 4 of 18 days; collina in 4 of 16 days. Either raise paxDay, cut the day limits, or accept that the levels are a sandbox. Note the simulation does not charge for the clicks a player spends drawing a line, so treat this as an upper bound on how easy the goal is.
Skill gap is 27 pointsGreedy wins 100%, random 73%, passive 0%. Decisions matter, which is the property that makes the tycoon worth playing.
