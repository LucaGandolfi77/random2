Three steps

How to play

  1. 1
    Load the colour

    The hopper pours one colour at a time. Tap that colour in the tray to put a bucket on the loop. The loop turns the matching bucket under the stream.

  2. 2
    Keep it moving

    A bucket fills and leaves automatically. Load another before the tray fills up. If the loop is full, tap a bucket to recycle it.

  3. 3
    Reveal the picture

    Every captured grain takes a pixel from the bottom three rows. The pixels above drop one row, so the photo is revealed from the top. Sand you miss falls into the spill tray.

Numbers and patterns on every cup are there so the game works without colour vision. Colours are labelled 1–8.

Dimmed cups are buried. They surface as the picture drops, then they become available.

No timer. Pause whenever you like. The only pressure is the spill tray.

Your photos stay here. The last one is remembered as a small local thumbnail and can be forgotten from the menu.
