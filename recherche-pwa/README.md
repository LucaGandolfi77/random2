# A la recherche du temps perdu — A Proustian Visual Novel

A contemplative interactive experience based on Marcel Prousts masterpiece. Memory, time, art, involuntary recollection, and the search for lost time are explored through choice, contemplation, and the melancholic beauty of language — in English and Italian.

## Theme

Melancholic and deep. This is not an adventure game — it is a meditation. Each choice deepens your contemplation of memory, art, and the passage of time. The atmosphere is contemplative: slow, poetic, and introspective.

## How to Play

Read the text slowly. Each passage is drawn from Prousts exploration of involuntary memory — the madeleine, the church steeple, the sound of the spoon against the cup. When choices appear:

- **Contemplate the memory** — deepen your relationship with the past
- **Proceed to the next recollection** — follow the path of time

Your statistics — Memory, Melancholy, Art — shift with each choice, shaping which ending you reach.

## Endings

Three possible conclusions, each reflecting a different philosophical position on time, memory, and art:
- A deep recollection of lost time through art
- A melancholic acceptance of time's irreversibility
- A redemptive moment through involuntary memory

## Languages

Fully bilingual. Switch at any time from the main menu:
- **English**: The original Proustian vocabulary
- **Italian**: A la ricerca — full Italian translation of all dialogue and interface

Fully offline-capable PWA. No external dependencies. Responsive. Screen-reader accessible. Synthesized audio. Save/load locally.

Based on Marcel Prousts A la recherche du temps perdu (1913-1927). A Proustian masterpiece — for those who seek depth, slowness, and the beauty of recollection.
