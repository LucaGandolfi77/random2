About this game

Join a red-haired machine hunter and a cheerful crew of heroes on a brick-built quest across forests, mountains and machine-haunted ruins. Play solo or share the adventure, decorate a village and uncover the secrets of a mysterious past.

A world built brick by brick

Venture into a distant future where nature has reclaimed the Earth and everything is made of interlocking bricks. Meet the Nora tribe in the settlement at the heart of the forest, then step beyond the village walls to find dinosaur-like machines grazing among the ruins.

The adventure of a lifetime

Take the role of a determined hunter fighting to save the world from an ancient digital demon and a sun-worshipping cult that would rather burn everything than live in the shade. The tone is playful, colourful and full of brick-built surprises.

Play alone or with friends

Hunt machines as the lead hero, or unlock colourful companions with their own skills and personalities. Bring a friend online or share a single screen for couch co-op — the world is always better together.

Make the village your own

Give the home settlement a makeover, decorating it until new buildings and ornaments appear. Dress the whole crew in ridiculous outfits, from armour to unlikely crossover costumes, and see what the community board has to say.

For newcomers and fans

Explore forests, mountains, underground cauldrons and towering climbing machines, all reimagined in bricks. Replay levels with new twists, chase better scores and dig deeper into the heroine's past.

Play Machine Hunt

An original top-down mini-game with a village hub, upgrades and companion bots. Drag to move, auto-fire, tap for a charged shot.

PlayStation Network account required

Linking a PlayStation Network account and an internet connection are required for some features. This demo does not create accounts or process payments.
