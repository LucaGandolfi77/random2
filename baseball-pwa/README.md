# DIAMOND POLY '99 — 3D Low-Poly Baseball PWA

A 3D low-poly mobile baseball arcade game built with zero external dependencies, custom flat-shaded 3D perspective polygon rendering, synthesized WebAudio stadium acoustics, and full offline support.

## Gameplay: Swing the Bat & Run the Bases!

1. **At-Bat (Aim & Swing)**:
   - Drag anywhere on the screen to position your sweet-spot reticle inside the 3D strike zone.
   - Choose between **Contact**, **Power**, or **Bunt** stances.
   - Release or tap **SWING** as the pitch (4-Seam Fastball, Slider, 12-6 Curve, Circle Change, or Splitter) crosses Home Plate.
2. **On the Basepaths (Sprint, Round & Slide)**:
   - Once the ball is in play, the 3D camera swoops to an overhead Stadium Broadcast view.
   - Tap **SPRINT** (or tap the screen / `Space`) to pump your runner's legs and kick up low-poly dirt clouds.
   - Tap **ROUND BASE (+1)** (or swipe up) to push for a double, triple, or inside-the-park home run!
   - Tap **DIVE / SLIDE!** (or swipe down) to slide under the tag on close plays at the bag.

## Game Modes

- **Pennant Match**: 3-inning game against 6 rival low-poly clubs across Day, Sunset, and Night stadiums.
- **Home Run Derby**: 10 outs to launch towering moonshots over the bleachers.
- **9th Inning Clutch**: Bottom of the 9th, bases loaded, 2 outs, down by 2 — drive them home to walk it off!
