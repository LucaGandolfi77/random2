    ABOUT

    Le Fil d'Or

    A twenty-minute animated adaptation of Victor Hugo's Les Misérables, composed entirely of code: ink, paper, candlelight and one continuous golden thread.

    Every frame is drawn live at 30 frames per second. Scrub, pause, switch language, or save a frame — the clock always returns the same image.

    Content note. The film depicts poverty, child labour, war, death and suicide. There is no graphic violence, and no rapid flashing.

    After Victor Hugo · 1862

    Written, drawn and scored in TypeScript. No images, footage or recorded music.
