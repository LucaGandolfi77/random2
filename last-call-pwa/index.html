READ THE ROOM

How to play

  1. Buzz is your pour. It fills over time to 10 and doubles in the last minute. Cards cost 1–6 Buzz.
  2. Drag a card onto your half of the bar (or tap a card, then the bar). Spells can land anywhere; everything else goes on your side.
  3. Your drinks walk themselves to the nearest coaster bridge, then attack the nearest tap or the Cellar. Destroy the Cellar to win.
  4. Protect your taps. Lose one and the rival unlocks a forward deploy zone. Lose the Cellar and the match is over.
  5. Three minutes, then Happy Hour. If the crowns are level, overtime starts: the first tower to fall wins.
  6. Upgrade what you use. Tips come from every match; card levels are permanent and local.
