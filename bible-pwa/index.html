About This Work

This is a very long and accurate adaptation of the Holy Bible as a contemplative visual novel. Every major book is represented with accurate verses, themes, and narrative flow — in both English and Italian. It is designed as a slow, meditative reading experience rather than an action game. Memory statistics track your journey through Scripture.
