About This Work

This contemplative visual novel adapts key movements of the Holy Bible — Creation, the Abrahamic Covenant, the Exodus and Red Sea, the Psalms, the Gospels, Acts, and the Apocalypse of John — in both English and Italian, accompanied by procedural low-poly sacred geometry and synthesized harmonies.
