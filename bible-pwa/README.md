# The Bible — A Very Long and Accurate Visual Novel of Scripture

Based on the Holy Bible. A contemplative visual experience covering Genesis through Revelation, in both English and Italian. Very long, accurate, and deeply melancholic.

## How to Experience

Read the Scripture passage that appears. Each chapter is drawn from the actual biblical text. When choices appear, reflect on the text or proceed to the next passage. The journey moves through all major books of Scripture: Genesis, Exodus, Psalms, the Gospels, Acts, and Revelation.

Your journey is tracked: Memory (understanding), Melancholy (the weight of the human condition), and Art (the beauty of the Word). The experience is designed to be slow — read it as you would meditate on a passage rather than play a game.

## Featured Books

- Genesis — Creation, fall, flood, covenant, Joseph
- Exodus — Slavery, liberation, the sea, the law, the wilderness
- Psalms — Prayer, lament, praise, the messianic hope
- Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, John) — Birth, ministry, passion, resurrection
- Acts — The Spirit, witness, journeys of faith
- Revelation — The throne, the seals, the new creation

## Technical Details

Full PWA: manifest, service worker, responsive design, bilingual, device-local save/load, synthesized scripture audio, no external dependencies. Works completely offline after installation.

A very long, accurate, contemplative visual novel of the Holy Bible — for those who seek the deepest questions of existence through the text of Scripture.
