# 15 — Legal and Privacy

## Before launch

- Trademark search for **CREW OF ONE**; register if clear and budget allows.
- Business entity, bank account, accounting.
- Terms of Service.
- Privacy Policy.
- Refund Policy (web) and platform-compliant IAP terms.
- EULA if required by stores.
- Age rating: likely 4+/Everyone; no violence, no ads, no UGC beyond moderated nicknames.
- Contractor agreements with full commercial rights assignment for art/audio.
- Font, tool, and sample licenses (OFL, MIT, commercial).
- AI disclosure per store policy.

## Web checkout

- Lemon Squeezy is merchant of record; handles VAT/sales tax.
- Clear price, currency, and refund terms before checkout.
- License key delivery and restore path.
- No dark patterns, no pre-checked upsells.

## Mobile IAP

- Apple/Google IAP only for digital goods.
- No external payment links on iOS.
- Restore purchases button.
- Parental gate if required by rating/store.
- No misleading “free” claims.

## Privacy

- Anonymous player ID; no account required.
- No IP storage in the app database.
- Optional nickname; profanity filter; report/block.
- Purchase email only for entitlement; deletion on request.
- Cookieless analytics where possible.
- GDPR/CCPA deletion and access path.
- No third-party ad trackers.
- Children’s privacy: avoid PII; if targeting under-13, comply with COPPA.

## Leaderboard moderation

- Default names like “Thief-1234.”
- Profanity filter.
- Report button.
- Human review of reports.
- Ban policy for cheating/harassment.
- Appeals email.

## AI policy

- AI may draft code, docs, QA, marketing, and concepts.
- Final art/audio human-made or commissioned.
- Keep an asset ledger: tool, version, license, human edits.
- Disclose AI usage where required by stores.
- Do not use AI outputs that infringe third-party IP.

## Data retention

- Runs/leaderboard: keep while the daily/season is active; archive after.
- Entitlements: keep while needed for restore.
- Analytics: aggregate, no PII.
- Deletion requests: 30 days.

## Support

- Support email.
- FAQ.
- Known issues.
- Save export/import instructions.
- Refund path.
