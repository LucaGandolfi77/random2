## Revenue reality

At $4.99 per bundle and ~70% net on web, you keep ~$3.50. At 30% store fee on mobile, ~$3.50. A $10k budget needs ~2,900 purchases to break even. A niche premium puzzle game can sell 1k–10k units with strong marketing. Treat this as a portfolio/commercial hybrid until the demo proves demand.

## Cost controls

- Prototype with rectangles before art.
- Commission a one-vault art slice before the full set.
- Use synthesized audio.
- Keep the backend on Cloudflare’s cheap tiers.
- No paid user acquisition until conversion is proven.
- Use AI for code/docs/QA, humans for final art/audio.

## Revenue paths

1. In-game chapter/bundle purchases.
2. Cosmetics + supporter pack.
3. Web checkout (better margin).
4. Mobile IAP (discovery + convenience).
5. Stretch: Apple Arcade / Netflix Games / Play Pass pitch.
6. Stretch: sponsored demo with a web game portal (after launch).
