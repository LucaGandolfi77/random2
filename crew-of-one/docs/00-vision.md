# 00 — Vision

## One line

A one-screen time-loop heist where your past runs become your crew.

## Elevator pitch

Every vault fits on one screen. You have 30 seconds and a watch that rewinds time. Each rewind leaves an echo of your last run — a ghost that repeats everything you did. The vault needs four people. You are one person with five loops. Plan the choreography. Pull the perfect heist.

## The hook

Most time-rewind games erase your mistakes. **Crew of One hires them.**

## Audience

- Players who like compact puzzle games: *Framed*, *Monument Valley*, *Hitman Go*, *Gorogoa*, *Into the Breach*.
- Mobile players who want a 5–10 minute session with no ads, no energy, and no twitch reflexes.
- The daily-challenge audience that enjoys a shared puzzle and a leaderboard.

## Platforms

1. Web/PWA at launch.
2. Android soft launch.
3. iOS full launch.

## Business model

Free download. Free prologue (3 vaults) and a free daily heist forever. Revenue comes only from in-game microtransactions:

- Chapter unlocks.
- Full-campaign bundle.
- Cosmetic ghost trails, watch skins, and UI themes.
- Supporter pack.

No ads. No energy. No loot boxes. No pay-to-win. No external merch.

## Differentiators

- **The crew is you.** The time-loop mechanic is the fantasy, the puzzle, and the marketing line.
- **One screen, no scrolling.** Everything is legible at once; the game feels like a board game.
- **Touch-native.** One thumb, floating joystick, tap to interact. No virtual D-pad.
- **Shareable runs.** The daily share card and “Ghost of the Day” replay make the game social without real-time netcode.
- **Server-validated leaderboard.** The shared deterministic sim makes cheating hard without a heavyweight anti-cheat stack.

## Pillars

1. **One screen, one thumb, one heist.**
2. **Failure is a crew member.**
3. **Ghosts are helpers, never obstacles.**
4. **Readable at a glance.**
5. **The rewind is the reward.**

## Success looks like

- A playtester understands the game in 10 seconds and says “oh!” in 90.
- The demo converts because the player wants more vaults, not because of a timer.
- The daily heist brings players back without feeling like a chore.
- The game looks like a commercial pixel-art product at phone size.
- The codebase can be maintained by one human plus agents.

## Working title

**CREW OF ONE** — tagline: *The crew is you.*

Final vault: “After You.” The title is the thesis: one person, many selves.

## Non-goals for v1

- Real-time multiplayer.
- A level editor.
- Cloud saves.
- A 3D or side-view camera.
- Narrative cutscenes or voice acting.
- Ads, energy, gacha, or loot boxes.
