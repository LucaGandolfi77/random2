# ADR 0003 — Safe AI art policy

## Status
Accepted.

## Context
AI can accelerate concepts and production, but final asset provenance and store disclosure matter for a commercial launch.

## Decision
AI may draft concepts, moodboards, UI mockups, marketing images, code, docs, QA, and level candidates. Final pixel art, animation, logo, key art, music, and SFX are human-made or commissioned. Keep an asset ledger.

## Consequences
- Higher art cost than raw AI generation.
- Lower IP/review risk.
- Clear commercial rights.
- Consistent visual quality.
