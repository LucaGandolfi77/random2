# 18 — Open Questions

## Product

- Is 15 vaults enough for launch, or should we add 5 challenge vaults?
- Should the daily heist be free forever, or free for the prologue only?
- Do we need a “hint” currency, or are free hints enough?
- Is the final vault title “After You” the right payoff?

## Monetization

- Bundle $3.99 or $4.99 at launch?
- Chapter-by-chapter, or bundle only?
- Which cosmetics sell best: ghost trails, watch skins, or UI themes?
- Should the supporter pack include the OST as a download?
- Do we test a single “Full Unlock” IAP after soft launch?

## Platform

- Web checkout at launch confirmed. Do we also allow license restore on mobile, or keep purchases separate?
- Android first, then iOS—or simultaneous?
- Do we wrap with Capacitor or build a native shell?
- Is Apple Arcade / Netflix Games worth pitching after traction?

## Content

- Do we need a level editor in v1.1, or is “Send a Ghost” enough?
- How many daily modifiers can we ship without new vaults?
- Do we add a story mode with narration, or keep it text-only?

## Team

- Who is the pixel artist? Commission or asset pack?
- Who is the sound designer?
- Do we hire a lawyer for terms/privacy, or use templates?
- What is the hard launch date and budget ceiling?

## AI

- Which agents/tools are allowed to touch production?
- What is the human review SLA?
- Do we disclose AI usage on the store page?
- How do we audit AI-generated code for security?

## Business

- What is the break-even target?
- Do we need a company entity before launch?
- What is the refund policy on web?
- Do we need age-gating for leaderboard nicknames?
