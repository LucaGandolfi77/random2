Three steps

How to play

  1. 1
    Load a colour

    Tap a palette colour to send its ants down a rail. Ants can only bite pixels on the exposed edge of the picture. One ant carries one pixel.

  2. 2
    Open the picture

    Every stolen pixel leaves a hole. Holes expose the pixels beside them, so the picture erodes inward from all four sides. If a colour is walled in, load another colour to cut a route to it.

  3. 3
    Clear the frame

    A bucket closes when it is full, when its colour is gone, or when you tap it to recall. The frame is empty only after every pixel reaches a bucket. There is no timer and no spill tray — only holes.

Numbers and patterns on every cup and bucket keep the game playable without colour vision.

Recall a bucket by tapping it on the rail. Its ants finish their trip, so carried pixels are never lost.

The edge is the only door. A colour with no exposed pixel waits until another bucket opens a route.

Your photos stay here. The last one is remembered as a small local thumbnail and can be forgotten from the menu.
