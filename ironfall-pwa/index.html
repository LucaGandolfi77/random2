IRONFALL

Three lanes to the Reliquary.

A 3v3 lane MOBA in the Warhammer 40k universe. Last-hit the wave, break the Bastion Towers, take the Reliquary, kill the Core. Eight champions, four warbands, one free browser game.

ENEMY DOCTRINE
HOW A LANE IS WON
  • Last-hit for gold. Assisting pays a third of it, XP is shared by everyone standing there.
  • Bastion Towers have three plates. Each plate is 62 armour. Lose all three and the tower drops to 30 — that is your dive window.
  • Plates regrow, faster before level 11 than after.
  • Push with your wave. A wave that arrives alone gets eaten.
  • Both Cores exposed means 25% more damage. That is the win clock.
CONTROLS
  • Left thumb — move. The stick appears where you touch and follows your thumb if you drag past the ring.
  • Right thumb — drag to aim, release, then tap an ability. The aim marker stays put after you lift your finger.
  • Ability pads — hold, not tap. An ability on cooldown fires the instant it comes up if you are still holding it.
  • Minimap tap — ping. FORGE and RECALL only work at your own bastion, and only out of combat.
  • Keyboard also works: WASD to move, 1–4 to cast, B to recall, Esc to pause.

CHOOSE YOUR CHAMPION

Eight champions. Four warbands. One pick each — your two allies and the enemy three are drafted around yours.

RUNES
